Formulation Philosophy

The science is in the formula.

Skincare does not need louder claims. It needs better formulation.

At Matilda Jane, every ingredient has a clear role. Each formula is built around skin function, ingredient compatibility, and research-backed actives used at percentages that work — not just to look good on a label.

If a synthetic ingredient is used, it is there for a clear reason — such as stability, preservation, skin feel, or formula performance — and it is clearly disclosed.

Ingredients

Natural where it makes sense.

Natural does not automatically mean better.

At Matilda Jane, natural and naturally derived ingredients are prioritised where they genuinely support the formula. Every ingredient is chosen for function, compatibility, safety, and performance — not just because it sounds good on a label.

If a synthetic ingredient is used, it is there for a clear reason, such as stability, preservation, skin feel, or formula performance, and it is clearly disclosed.

How we formulate

Built differently.

Function-first formulas

Every ingredient is selected for a reason — whether it supports hydration, barrier comfort, texture, stability, preservation, or the delivery of key actives.

Designed around skin biology

Formulas are developed with the skin barrier, pH, moisture balance, and skin feel in mind.

No token actives

Hero ingredients are not added just to look good on a label. Actives are chosen for their purpose, evidence, compatibility, and role within the complete formula.

Transparency

What's on the label is in the formula.

Matilda Jane uses clear ingredient choices and transparent formulation logic.

That means every ingredient is selected for a purpose. Actives are chosen for their role in the formula, botanicals are used where they genuinely support the product, and natural or naturally derived ingredients are prioritised where they make sense.

Synthetic ingredients are always disclosed clearly, with their function explained where useful. Preservative systems are chosen for product safety, stability, and suitability.
