The Philosophy

Skincare that works because the science is real.

Most skincare is built around a hero ingredient and a marketing story. Matilda Jane is built differently — every formulation begins with a question: what actually changes the skin?

Peer-reviewed research. Actives at the concentrations shown to work in published trials. Barrier respected at every step. Products that earn results — not promise them.

Made in New Zealand

Formulated and produced in New Zealand using globally sourced clinical-grade actives. Clinical concentrations, evidence-based decisions, and a barrier-first philosophy in every product. The best of nature — refined.

Built on Evidence

Every active is dosed at the concentration shown to work in published, peer-reviewed clinical trials. Bakuchiol at 1%. Neurophroline™ at 2%. Cacay Oil at 5%. Real science, real percentages — not marketing claims.

Personal Formulations
