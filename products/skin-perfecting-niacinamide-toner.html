What is it?

A hydrating step with active percentages that do something.

A water-light toner that goes on after cleansing and before your serum.

It is not a stripping, astringent-heavy toner. It is a hydrating step with active percentages that do something — 5% niacinamide and 2% alpha arbutin in a base of witch hazel and lemon myrtle hydrosols, with skin's own moisture factors built back in.

What does it do?

Corrects tone in a hydrating base — not a drying one.

Helps improve the appearance of uneven tone and dark spots. Niacinamide at 5% is the concentration used in published clinical work on skin tone and texture.1 2 Alpha arbutin supports this by slowing the enzyme that drives melanin production.3

Because the formula corrects tone in a hydrating base rather than a drying one, two things follow:

Skin holds hydration better

Sodium PCA and betaine are Natural Moisturising Factors — the water-binding compounds your skin already makes, and makes less of over time. Adding them back helps skin feel supple rather than tight.

The surface looks smoother and less dull

Two molecular weights of hyaluronic acid work at different depths: the larger sits on the surface for immediate cushion, the smaller draws hydration further in.4

Who it's for

Brightening without the drying.

Anyone working on uneven tone, dark spots or dullness who does not want a drying toner to get there.

It suits oily and combination skin — niacinamide helps with the look of pores and shine — and equally suits drier or mature skin, where natural moisture factors decline and hydration matters more.

If your skin is highly sensitive or reactive, patch test first. This formula contains citral, which occurs naturally in lemon myrtle. Or contact Matilda Jane for individual product guidance.

How to use · Step 2

After cleansing, before serum.

Dispense 3–4 drops into clean hands and pat evenly over damp skin. Do not use a cotton pad; patting keeps the actives on your skin rather than on the pad.

Follow with serum and moisturiser while skin is still slightly damp. AM and PM. Leave on — do not rinse.

Pro tips

Apply to damp skin. Humectants like hyaluronic acid and sodium PCA pull water from whatever is nearest. Damp skin gives them something to work with.
Follow with moisturiser. A toner this light needs something over it to seal the hydration in, particularly if your skin is dry.
Wear SPF daily. Any work you do on dark spots is undone by unprotected sun exposure. This matters more than the product itself.

Good to know

FAQs

Will this dry my skin out like other toners? ▾

No. There is no alcohol added, and the formula is built around hydration. Witch hazel provides a light astringent effect without the stripping.

Can I use it with vitamin C? ▾

Yes — apply vitamin C first, then this. They work on pigment through different pathways.

Can I use it with retinol or exfoliating acids? ▾

Yes. Use this toner first. If your skin is new to actives, alternate nights rather than layering everything at once.

Why does it smell lemony? ▾

Lemon myrtle hydrosol. There is no added fragrance and no essential oil.

How long until I see a difference? ▾

Tone and dark spots respond slowly. Published trials on these actives run 8–12 weeks. Hydration you should feel immediately.

Storage ▾

Store cool and out of direct sunlight. Keep the cap closed. Use clean hands each time. Best within 12 months of opening.
