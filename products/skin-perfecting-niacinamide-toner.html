At a Glance

  • Skin concerns: uneven tone, dark spots, dullness, dehydration.
  • Skin type: all skin types, including oily, combination, dry and mature (patch test if your skin is sensitive or reactive).
  • Scent: natural lemon-myrtle scent — no added fragrance, no essential oil.
  • Texture: water-light toner that absorbs immediately.

What is it?

A water-light toner that goes on after cleansing and before your serum. Not a stripping, astringent-heavy toner — a hydrating step with active percentages that actually do something. 5% niacinamide and 2% alpha arbutin sit in a base of witch hazel and lemon myrtle hydrosols, with skin's own moisture factors built back in.

What does it do?

Helps improve the appearance of uneven tone and dark spots. Niacinamide at 5% is the concentration used in published clinical work on skin tone and texture, and alpha arbutin supports it by slowing the enzyme that drives melanin production. Because it corrects tone in a hydrating base rather than a drying one, Natural Moisturising Factors and two weights of hyaluronic acid leave skin feeling supple rather than tight, and the surface looking smoother and less dull.

Niacinamide 5%

Vitamin B3 — the hero of the formula. Supports the skin barrier, helps refine the look of pores, and helps even the appearance of skin tone. Used at 5% because that is the level published research has actually tested — not a token inclusion.

Alpha Arbutin 2%

Works on pigment at the source by inhibiting tyrosinase, the enzyme that triggers melanin production. A gentler, more stable route to brightening than many alternatives. Helps fade the look of dark spots over time.

Natural Moisturising Factors + Dual-Weight HA

Sodium PCA and betaine are the water-binding compounds your skin already makes, and makes less of over time. Two molecular weights of hyaluronic acid then work at different depths — the larger cushions the surface, the smaller draws hydration further in.

Step 2 — after cleansing, before serum. Use morning and night.

Routine order:
Cleanse → Skin Perfecting Toner → Serum → Moisturiser

Dispense 3–4 drops into clean hands and pat evenly over damp skin. Do not use a cotton pad — patting keeps the actives on your skin rather than on the pad. Follow with serum and moisturiser while skin is still slightly damp. Leave on — do not rinse.

Pro Tips

  • Apply to damp skin — humectants pull water from whatever is nearest, so damp skin gives them something to work with.
  • Always follow with moisturiser to seal the hydration in, particularly if your skin is dry.
  • Wear SPF daily — any work on dark spots is undone by unprotected sun exposure.

Storage

Store cool and out of direct sunlight. Keep the cap closed and use clean hands each time. Best within 12 months of opening.

Cautions

For external use only. Patch test before use. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children. Contains citral, naturally present in lemon myrtle — patch test if your skin is sensitive or reactive.
