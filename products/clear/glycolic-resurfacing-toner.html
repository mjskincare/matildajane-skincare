At a Glance

  • Skin concerns: dullness, uneven texture, rough skin, congestion, fine lines.
  • Skin type: normal to combination/oily (patch test if you have sensitive or reactive skin; avoid during pregnancy or breastfeeding).
  • Scent: scent-free.
  • Texture: lightweight, water-thin toner.

What is it?

A 5% glycolic acid toner formulated at pH 3.6–3.9 to deliver measured exfoliation while supporting the skin barrier. Kakadu plum extract contributes natural vitamin C, and pro-vitamin B5 helps maintain comfort and barrier function during use.

What does it do?

Resurfaces, smooths and brightens. Glycolic acid is the smallest of the alpha hydroxy acids — at 5% and pH 3.6–3.9 approximately half the acid sits in its free, active form, the form that loosens the bonds between dead surface cells. Use builds smoother, more even skin over weeks of consistent application. Kakadu plum, the highest known natural source of vitamin C, supports the brightening effect through a separate antioxidant pathway. Panthenol, sodium lactate and betaine act together to maintain comfort and reduce the sting acids can sometimes cause.

Glycolic Acid 5%

The smallest AHA, with the highest skin penetration of all alpha hydroxy acids. At pH 3.6–3.9 approximately half the acid sits in its free, active form — the form that loosens dead surface cells. Use builds smoother, more even skin over weeks of consistent application.

Kakadu Plum Extract

Sourced from Terminalia ferdinandiana, kakadu plum holds the highest natural concentration of vitamin C of any known fruit. Provides antioxidant support and complements glycolic acid's brightening pathway without adding additional exfoliation.

Pro-Vitamin B5

Also known as panthenol. Helps skin hold onto moisture, supports the skin barrier, and reduces the sensation of sting acids can cause. Important during AHA use.

Use 2–3 nights per week to start, building to nightly as your skin adapts. Evening use only.

Routine order:
Cleanse → Glycolic Resurfacing Toner → Hydrating Serum → Moisturiser

Apply to a cotton pad and sweep over clean, dry skin. Avoid the eye area. Follow with moisturiser. Sunscreen is required during the day and for one week after stopping use.

Pro Tips

  • Start slowly — twice a week is enough at the beginning.
  • Always apply sunscreen the following morning.
  • Pair with a barrier-supporting moisturiser to keep skin comfortable.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Sunburn Alert

SUNBURN ALERT: This product contains an alpha hydroxy acid (AHA) that may increase your skin's sensitivity to the sun and particularly the possibility of sunburn. Use a sunscreen, wear protective clothing, and limit sun exposure while using this product and for a week afterwards.

Cautions

Patch test before first use. For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue if irritation occurs. Avoid during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Not for use on broken or compromised skin.
