Barrier-first hydration.

Hydration is one function. A well-formulated moisturiser does more.

Matilda Jane moisturisers are built barrier-first — formulated to restore and maintain the acid mantle, support lipid replenishment, and reduce TEWL. Clinically studied actives are selected and adjusted to suit specific skin concerns, at concentrations where research supports their effectiveness. The result: a moisturiser that addresses the underlying function of healthy skin — not just the surface feel of it.

Face Moisturiser FAQs

Common questions about choosing the right Matilda Jane moisturiser.

Start with the primary concern. Dry or compromised barrier — the Calm Collection. Visible aging combined with breakout-prone skin — the Renew Collection. Hyperpigmentation or dullness — the Brighten Collection.

Every Matilda Jane moisturiser is barrier-first by design. The choice comes down to which actives address the specific concern.

Dry skin needs lipid replenishment, not just water. The Calming Barrier Face Cream from the Calm Collection is built around colloidal oat USP, niacinamide and squalane to restore lipid structure and reduce TEWL — light enough for daytime, layered enough for very compromised skin overnight.

The Neuro-Restore Face Crème is engineered for skin that's both aging and blemish-prone — a combination most "anti-aging" formulas refuse to address. Cacay oil is non-comedogenic, bakuchiol regulates cell turnover without irritation, and the formula is free of pore-clogging waxes and heavy butters.

Neuro-Restore Face Crème. Bakuchiol delivers retinol-equivalent collagen results without sensitisation. Neurophroline™ blocks cortisol-driven collagen breakdown. Cacay Oil supports cellular renewal — three times the natural retinol of rosehip. All at clinically validated concentrations.

A compromised barrier loses water (TEWL increases), reacts more easily, and makes every other active less effective. Restoring and maintaining the acid mantle is the foundation everything else builds on. Hydration without barrier support is short-term. Barrier support without hydration leaves skin tight. A well-formulated moisturiser does both.
