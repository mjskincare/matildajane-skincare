An eye gel formulated to target the three pathways known to cause discolouration and puffiness around the eye.

Vascular. The most common type. Haemoglobin pools beneath thin periorbital skin and oxidises to dark pigments — creating the characteristic blue, purple or red discolouration. REGU-AGE® improves microcirculation and protects the capillary matrix through enzyme inhibition. Hesperidin Methyl Chalcone (orange peel extract) strengthens capillary walls and directly inhibits the oxidation of haemoglobin to the compounds that cause discolouration. Encapsulated caffeine promotes vasoconstriction to reduce visible vessel engorgement.

Pigmentation. Melanin accumulates in the periorbital skin — common after UV exposure, inflammation, or in darker skin tones. Niacinamide inhibits melanosome transfer: the step at which melanin moves from melanocytes to surrounding skin cells. Clinical studies confirm 35–68% inhibition of this transfer.

Structural. Weakened connective tissue and fluid retention create puffiness and deepen the shadow beneath the eye. REGU-AGE® inhibits collagenase and elastase — the enzymes that break down the connective tissue matrix. Pep-Eye® (Acetyl Tetrapeptide-5) reduces capillary permeability through ACE inhibition to limit fluid accumulation, and inhibits glycation of structural proteins to preserve the collagen network.

Anyone with dark circles, puffiness or discolouration around the eye — or targeted dark patches elsewhere on the face. Suitable for all skin types. Fragrance-free.

  • REGU-AGE® — a complex of purified soy and rice peptides with biotechnologically produced Superoxide Dismutase. Targets periorbital dark circles through free-radical neutralisation, collagenase and elastase inhibition, and measurable improvement in capillary microcirculation. In a double-blind in vivo study, 5% REGU-AGE® produced a 32% reduction in dark circles and 25% reduction in puffiness at four weeks.
  • Hesperidin Methyl Chalcone — a bioflavonoid derived from citrus (orange) peel that works directly on the capillary endothelium. Reduces capillary filtration and inhibits the oxidation of leaked haemoglobin to biliverdin and bilirubin — the compounds that create the blue-purple colour of vascular dark circles. Addresses the colour at its source.
  • Pep-Eye® (Acetyl Tetrapeptide-5) — a synthetic tetrapeptide developed specifically for the under-eye area. Reduces capillary permeability through ACE inhibition and inhibits protein glycation to preserve the skin's structural network. Clinical data: 70% of subjects showed ≥30% puffiness volume reduction at 15 days; 95% showed measurable improvement at 60 days.
  • Encapsulated Caffeine — caffeine delivered via cyclodextrin encapsulation for enhanced penetration. Works through adenosine receptor antagonism and phosphodiesterase inhibition to promote lymphatic drainage and reduce visible puffiness.
  • Niacinamide — addresses the pigment-pathway component of dark circles by inhibiting the transfer of melanin-containing melanosomes to neighbouring keratinocytes (35–68% inhibition in clinical models). Formulated at 2%, calibrated for the thinner periorbital skin.

Morning and evening, as the last step before eye cream, moisturiser or SPF.

Pro-Tips

  • Use your ring finger — it applies the least pressure.
  • Pat, don't rub.
  • Use morning and evening consistently — results build over weeks.

Storage: store in a cool, dry place away from heat and direct sunlight.

Cautions: patch test before first use. For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes — if contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water. Apply to the orbital bone area only; do not apply to the eyelid or within 1cm of the lash line. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

Dark circles have multiple overlapping causes: blood pooling and haemoglobin oxidation under thin skin (vascular), melanin accumulation at the surface (pigment), weakened capillary and connective tissue structure, and fluid retention causing puffiness. This gel targets all five — vascular, oxidative, structural, fluid accumulation, and melanin — simultaneously using five distinct actives.

Clinical data for the key actives shows visible improvement from 15 days with consistent twice-daily use, and a 32% reduction in dark circles at four weeks for REGU-AGE®. Vascular mechanisms respond relatively quickly; pigment changes take longer. Use consistently morning and evening.
