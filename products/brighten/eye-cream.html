A light, fast-absorbing cream for the skin around the eyes. Hydrates and softens the look of dark circles, puffiness, and fine lines.

100% natural. COSMOS-approved ingredients. No synthetic fragrances. Best for all skin types — especially dry and maturing skin. Lightweight, non-greasy finish.

  • Hydrates and smooths the eye area — glycerin and hyaluronic acid bind water for lasting surface hydration.
  • Helps reduce the look of puffiness — caffeine constricts blood vessels and brightens.
  • Improves the appearance of uneven tone and dullness — niacinamide and sodium ascorbyl phosphate work together for radiance.
  • Softens and comforts the skin barrier — hemisqualane and plant oils nourish without heaviness.

Daily, AM and PM.

After cleansing, apply a small amount to the skin around the eyes morning and night. Allow to absorb before SPF or makeup. Always patch test first.

Pro-Tips

  • Tap, don't rub — use your ring finger to gently press the cream into the skin around the eyes; the skin here is delicate and reacts well to a soft tap.
  • Cool before use — store in the fridge for an extra de-puffing kick on tired mornings.

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Best used within 6 months of opening.
