Three toners, each formulated for its own job. The Niacinamide Refining Toner is the daily option — gentle enough for AM and PM use, formulated at pH 5.2–5.5 to keep niacinamide stable and active. The Glycolic Resurfacing Toner and Salicylic Clarifying Toner are treatment options for 2–3 nights per week — glycolic for surface renewal, salicylic for pore decongestion.

Three different mechanisms, three different jobs. Use one on its own, or build a layered routine across all three.

How to layer them

Daily base. Treatment swaps.

The Niacinamide Refining Toner is your daily base. The acid toners replace it on treatment nights — they don't layer with it.

Standard treatment routine:

  • Daily AM and PM: Niacinamide Refining Toner
  • Monday + Wednesday PM: replace Niacinamide with Salicylic Clarifying Toner
  • Friday PM: replace Niacinamide with Glycolic Resurfacing Toner
  • Always apply SPF the morning after using an acid toner

Use just one toner from the collection, or build a layered routine across all three.
