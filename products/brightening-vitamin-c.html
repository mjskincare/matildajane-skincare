pH Window

Vitamin C is only active between pH 3.5 and 4.0.

Above pH 4.0, ascorbic acid is inactive. Below pH 3.0, it's irritating. The narrow window between is where the magic happens — and where most "brightening" serums fail.

We buffer with sodium hydroxide and phytate to lock the formula in that window for the life of the bottle.

Native Power

Kakadu Plum — 100× the vitamin C of an orange.

Indigenous to Northern Australia, Kakadu Plum holds the highest natural concentration of vitamin C of any known fruit. Used for centuries by Aboriginal communities; only recently understood by modern dermatology.

Tone-Evening

Originally developed in Japanese dermatology to treat melasma, tranexamic acid blocks the melanocyte signalling pathway rather than just brightening surface pigment.
