Real percentages. Disclosed concentrations. This formula uses one disclosed hero active. Carrier oils, supporting oils, tocopherol and essential oils support the base; their percentages are not displayed in line with brand convention.

  • Coenzyme Q10 2% — a lipid-soluble antioxidant naturally present in skin. Levels decline with age and UV exposure. Used here to support antioxidant defence and the look of smoother, stronger, more resilient skin.1,2
  • Sea Buckthorn Fruit Oil — bright orange botanical oil from Hippophae rhamnoides. Naturally rich in carotenoids, tocopherols, fatty acids and phytosterols. Supports antioxidant care, softness and visible radiance.
  • Rosehip Seed Oil — lightweight botanical oil rich in linoleic and alpha-linolenic acids. Supports skin softness and barrier comfort while balancing the richer oils with a lighter skin feel.
  • Camellia, Squalane & Meadowfoam — the lightweight oil base. Camellia gives elegant spread; squalane keeps the feel non-greasy; meadowfoam helps support oxidative stability.
  • Frankincense & Lavender Essential Oils — give the formula its natural aromatic profile and add skin-comfort support. Not used as synthetic fragrance. May not suit very sensitive skin or those who avoid essential oils.

Daily, preferably PM.

Pump a small amount onto fingertips. Warm between palms. Press gently into face and neck, avoiding the immediate eye area. Use after water-based serums and before moisturiser, or mix a small amount into moisturiser for a lighter finish.

  • Use once daily — best for most skin types, especially normal, combination, or skin new to facial oils.
  • Can use twice daily — dry or mature skin may use morning and evening if well tolerated.
  • Use less often — combination or congestion-prone skin should start every second night, or mix a small amount into moisturiser.
  • Avoid — very oily or acne-prone skin may find this formula too rich. Avoid if sensitive to lavender, frankincense, essential oils or listed fragrance allergens.

Pro-Tips

  • Layering — apply after hydrating serums and before moisturiser.
  • Mix-in boost — add a small amount to your favourite moisturiser for extra comfort and glow.
  • Eye area — keep away from the lash line, eyelids and direct under-eye area. If using near the eye, apply only to the orbital bone.
  • Storage — store cool, dry and away from heat and direct sunlight. Keep tightly closed. CoQ10 is light- and oxygen-sensitive; the pump lid is designed to minimise both. Use within 6 months of opening.
  • Fabric care — concentrated CoQ10 is known to stain. The finished 2% oil is much lower risk, but take care around light-coloured clothing, towels and pillowcases until fully absorbed.

The Science Behind the Formula

Beyond the headline active — the choices that make this formula work.

01

CoQ10 Antioxidant Support

CoQ10 (ubiquinone) is a lipid-soluble antioxidant naturally present in skin and used in cosmetic formulas for skin-ageing support. This formula uses 2% Coenzyme Q10 to support antioxidant defence and the look of smoother, more resilient skin.

02

Sea Buckthorn Carotenoid Profile

Sea buckthorn fruit oil contains carotenoids, tocopherols and fatty acids. The golden colour is natural and expected. Included to support the antioxidant story without over-tinting the formula.

03

Lightweight Oil Architecture

Camellia, rosehip, meadowfoam and squalane create a soft, smooth facial oil without a heavy finish. Meadowfoam seed oil and tocopherol help support oxidative stability in the oil blend.

04

Anhydrous Format

An oil-based formula with no added water. A traditional water-phase preservative is not required. Tocopherol and meadowfoam support oxidative stability of the lipid system.

05

Honest Formulation

2% Coenzyme Q10. COSMOS-aligned ingredients. Every ingredient has a clear purpose and is fully disclosed. Always disclosed. Never disguised.
