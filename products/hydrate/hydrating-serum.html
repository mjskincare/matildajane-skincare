A lightweight hydrating serum for skin that looks dry, flat, tight or tired.

This formula combines three targeted cosmetic ingredients: triple hyaluronic acid for layered hydration and visible plumping, Mamaku black fern extract for immediate skin tightening and smoothing, and neroli hydrosol for a soft botanical water phase.

The 1.1% HA system uses three molecular weights to support hydration at different levels of the skin surface. High molecular weight HA helps form a hydrating film, while lower molecular weight HA can move further into the stratum corneum.

Mamaku extract is used at 3%, above the supplier's standard 1–2% use level. Supplier data describes a 3D viscoelastic matrix effect from glucuronomannans, helping give an immediate tightening feel.

Neroli hydrosol makes up 30% of the water phase. Aloe vera powder, sclerotium gum and glycerin support slip, hydration and a smooth skin feel.

Target pH: 5.3–5.5.
No added fragrance oil or essential oil.
Silicone-free.

  • Triple Hyaluronic Acid 1.1% — high, medium and low molecular weight sodium hyaluronate for layered surface hydration and visible plumping.
  • Mamaku 3% — Cyathea medullaris (NZ black fern) extract. Supplier data describes glucuronomannans forming a 3D viscoelastic matrix, with a reported immediate tightening effect.
  • Neroli Hydrosol 30% — Citrus aurantium amara flower water replaces part of the plain water phase, giving a botanical base and natural neroli scent.

Daily, AM and/or PM.

After cleansing and toning, apply 2–3 drops to slightly damp skin. Press gently into face and neck. Follow with moisturiser and SPF in the morning.

Pro-Tips

  • Mix-in boost — add 1–2 drops to your favourite moisturiser for an easy hydration boost.
  • Mamaku tightening — for the strongest tightening effect, apply the serum directly to skin first and let it set before moisturiser.
  • Damp skin — apply on slightly damp skin to maximise HA hydration.
