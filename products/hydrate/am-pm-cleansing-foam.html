What is it?

A dual morning and evening cleansing system built around gentle amino acid surfactants. Where conventional foaming cleansers use harsh sulphates that disrupt the skin's acid mantle, AM + PM Cleansing Foam is pH-balanced to 5.0–5.5 — the skin's natural range — and uses sodium cocoyl glutamate (derived from coconut and fermented sugar) to lift away dirt, oil and SPF without stripping.

What does it do?

Cleanses gently, supports the skin barrier, and leaves skin feeling soft, calm and hydrated — never tight, never squeaky.

  • Respects the acid mantle. pH 5.0–5.5 supports the skin's natural defence layer and helps preserve a balanced microbiome.1
  • Cleanses without stripping. Amino acid surfactants (sodium cocoyl glutamate) lift away oil, dirt and SPF while leaving the lipid barrier intact.2
  • Calms and softens during cleansing. Pro-vitamin B5 (panthenol) helps the skin hold onto water and supports a comfortable post-cleanse feel.3
  • Hydrates as it cleanses. Glycerin acts as a humectant, drawing water into the upper layers of the skin so it feels soft — not squeaky — once rinsed.4

Morning

Dispense 1–2 pumps onto damp skin. Massage gently in upward circles for 20–30 seconds, then rinse with lukewarm water. Follow with toner, serum, eye care, moisturiser and SPF.

Evening

If you've worn SPF, makeup or city pollution, double cleanse: start with an oil cleanser or balm to break down the day, then follow with PM Cleansing Foam to remove residue. Massage onto damp skin for 30–45 seconds and rinse. Continue with toner, treatment serums, eye care and night cream.

Routine order

Cleanse → Tone → Serum → Eye Care → Moisturiser → SPF (AM)
Cleanse → Tone → Treatment → Eye Care → Night Cream (PM)

Pro Tips

  • Always apply to damp skin — wet skin helps the surfactants disperse evenly and rinse cleanly.
  • Use lukewarm water — hot water strips the skin's natural oils and can leave skin feeling tight.
  • Don't over-cleanse. 20–30 seconds is enough in the morning; 30–45 seconds at night.
  • Pat dry with a clean towel and follow with toner or serum while skin is still slightly damp to lock in hydration.

Storage

Store upright in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. The pump dispenser keeps the formula sealed from air contamination — no refrigeration needed.

Cautions

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes — rinse thoroughly with water if contact occurs. Patch test before first use if you have sensitive skin. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Ingredient Spotlight

Amino Acid Surfactant · Pro-Vitamin B5 · Glycerin

Amino Acid Surfactant

Sodium cocoyl glutamate. Barrier-respecting cleanse.

Derived from coconut fatty acids and fermented sugar amino acids, sodium cocoyl glutamate is one of the mildest surfactants available. It cleanses by lifting away oil and dirt without disrupting the skin's lipid bilayer, making it suitable for daily use on dry, sensitive and barrier-impaired skin.

Pro-Vitamin B5

Panthenol. Calms and softens.

Panthenol converts to vitamin B5 (pantothenic acid) in the skin, where it acts as a humectant and supports barrier function. In a cleanser it helps offset any drying effect of the rinse-off process — skin feels comfortable and supple, not tight, once towel-dried.

Glycerin

Humectant. Draws water in.

Glycerin is one of the most studied humectants in skincare. Even in a rinse-off format, it helps draw water into the upper layers of the skin during cleansing, so the post-rinse feel is soft and hydrated rather than tight and stripped.
